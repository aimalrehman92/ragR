Course Objective and Description
This course is the first part of a two-course sequence. The objective of the course is to develop advanced-level understanding and working knowledge of statistical inference. The course focuses on statistical estimation theory. Topics include: review of probability concepts, method of moments, maximum likelihood estimation, minimum variance unbiased estimation, sufficiency and completeness, Fisher information and Cramer-Rao bounds, statistical decision theory, and Bayes decision rules.

Textbooks
- Casella & Berger (2002), Statistical Inference (2nd Edition), Cengage
- Shao, Jun (2003), Mathematical Statistics (2nd Edition), Springer

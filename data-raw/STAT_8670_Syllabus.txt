Materials

- Optional Textbooks:
  * Rizzo (2019). Statistical Computing with R.
  * Gelman et al. (2013). Bayesian Data Analysis.
  * Givens & Hoeting (2012). Computational Statistics.
  * Hothorn & Everitt (2006). Handbook of Statistical Analyses Using R.
- Software: R programming language and environment.

Course Objectives
Students will learn:
1. Programming fundamentals
2. Data manipulation
3. Numerical modeling and optimization
4. Stochastic simulation (Monte Carlo, variance reduction)
5. Monte Carlo inference (EM, Bootstrap, Permutation tests)
